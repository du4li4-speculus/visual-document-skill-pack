# Research Intelligence Project Template

## Purpose

A reusable research framework separating knowledge building from market intelligence.

## Modules

```
research-intelligence/

├── subject-knowledge/
│   Academic and domain expertise.
│
├── lesson-design/
│   Teaching preparation and material development.
│
├── market-research/
│   Industry, competitor, and opportunity analysis.
│
└── outputs/
    Reports, briefs, and decisions.
```

# Module A: Subject Knowledge & Lesson Preparation

## Goal

Build deep understanding before teaching or producing materials.

## Structure

```
subject-knowledge/

├── concepts/
├── frameworks/
├── examples/
├── misconceptions/
├── difficulty-map/
└── references/
```

## Lesson Design Flow

```
Knowledge
 ↓
Learning objective
 ↓
Student difficulty
 ↓
Teaching sequence
 ↓
Practice
 ↓
Assessment
```

Capture:

- what students need to understand
- common failure patterns
- prerequisite knowledge
- transferable skills
- explanation strategies

# Module B: Market Research

## Goal

Understand external opportunities and competitive landscape.

## Structure

```
market-research/

├── market-map/
├── competitors/
├── customer-needs/
├── trends/
├── opportunities/
└── evidence/
```

## Rules

- Separate facts from interpretation.
- Record source quality.
- Maintain competing explanations.
- Avoid collecting information without decisions.

## Suitable Projects

- AP/A-Level strategy
- education market analysis
- course positioning
- industry research
